About

Mary Nunes is a Copywriter based in New York City, with a focus on beauty, wellness, and lifestyle. She brings brand voices to life with a sharp point of view and creative edge.

Previously, she was the in-house copywriter at amika and a Senior Copywriter at Antonym, a brand voice and copy strategy studio. Her experience spans the entire product lifecycle—from naming, packaging, and creative concepting to in-store displays, digital, and social.

Mary is available for freelance copywriting projects across brand and editorial spaces.
